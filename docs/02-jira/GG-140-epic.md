# GG-140 Epic — Customer-initiated booking cancellation

**Type:** Epic  
**Parent:** none  
**Story points:** 13 (roll-up)

## Objective

Customers cancel eligible GarageGuru service bookings in product instead of through support, with one policy across Node, React, and Flutter.

## Success metrics

- Support tickets like SUP-8841 for “please cancel my booking” drop after release
- Cancelled bookings always release the garage slot
- Garage and assigned technician receive `booking.cancelled`
